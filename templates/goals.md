# Goals file

The Chief of Staff keeps this file (in its memory or a shared doc). The weekly goals meeting adds one
section per week. Newest week on top. Each project bot gets its own part copied into its brief under
"Goals right now".

Rules: one to three goals per project per week. Each goal has a "Done means" line anyone could check.
Scores are met, partly or missed, each with one line of evidence. Only goals the human confirmed go in.

---

## Week of YYYY-MM-DD (confirmed by OWNER on YYYY-MM-DD)

### PROJECT-ID (owner bot: BOT-ID)
1. GOAL IN ONE SENTENCE.
   Done means: WHAT ANYONE COULD CHECK.
2. GOAL.
   Done means: CHECK.

### PROJECT-ID (owner bot: BOT-ID)
1. GOAL.
   Done means: CHECK.

### Last week's scores
- PROJECT-ID 1. met: EVIDENCE (link or number).
- PROJECT-ID 2. partly: WHAT IS LEFT.
- PROJECT-ID 1. missed: WHY, IN ONE LINE.
